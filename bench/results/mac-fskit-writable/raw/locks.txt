# locks on the voidfs mount (final build)
  fcntl F_SETLK write lock                     ok
  another process's F_SETLK while held         refused (held)
  fcntl F_SETLK unlock                         ok
  another process's F_SETLK after unlock       granted
  flock LOCK_EX|LOCK_NB                        ok
  another process's flock while held           refused (held)
  another process's flock after LOCK_UN        granted
  open O_EXLOCK                                ok
  another process's O_EXLOCK while held        refused (held)
  another process's O_EXLOCK after close       granted
